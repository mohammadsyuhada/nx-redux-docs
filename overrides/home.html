What you get

  • Netplay with one button

    Press Y on a supported game to host or join over Wi-Fi or a hotspot. No IP addresses, and saves sync before the match.

  • Emulators included

    Nintendo 64, Nintendo DS and Dreamcast run on bundled standalone emulators, so there is nothing extra to download. The mobile app bundles every core.

    Handheld and Mobile

  • Resume any game

    Quitting saves to a hidden slot. Press SELECT for the Game Switcher and carry on exactly where you left off.

    Earn RetroAchievements without a connection and sync them later.

  • Sync your devices

    Add games on one device, then sync over Wi-Fi. Saves, states and, if you like, ROMs arrive on the other.
